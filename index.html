About

Thawng Ling is a Myanmar guitarist, music producer and musician, known for his work as a session guitarist, recording musician and music producer.

Born in Hlatui Village, Mindat Township, Chin State, Myanmar, he has built his musical career around the guitar and has collaborated with artists across Myanmar’s contemporary music scene.

His musical identity is centered on guitar performance and music production. From session guitar and acoustic performances to studio production and independent projects, he has contributed to a wide range of Myanmar music releases, working with artists including X-Boxin, Shwe Htoo, D-Vision, Ni Ni Khin Zaw, Shine, Ye’ Lay, Mary, Benjamin Sum and others.
